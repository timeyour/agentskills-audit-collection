## Purpose

Validate the second supplied list with a narrower question: which vibe-coded sites are actually useful for learning how to build sellable, client-ready, or productized websites?

This run prioritizes business structure over visual novelty.

## Monetizable Case Criteria

Each case is scored against five practical signals:

1. Business reality: real company, real professional service, real tool, or real template flow.
2. Conversion surface: CTA, form, ordering, RSVP, login, booking, contact, or checkout.
3. Operational depth: backend, database, CMS, admin, auth, content system, or reusable template logic.
4. Tool attribution: credible evidence that the case came from Lovable, v0, or Replit.
5. Reuse value: can a builder copy structure, positioning, content model, or product flow?

Verdicts:

- `PRIORITY`: strong evidence and strong reuse value for monetizable website work.
- `USEFUL WITH NOTES`: worth studying, but evidence, live access, or business depth is partial.
- `LOW PRIORITY`: visible and attributable, but mostly template/novelty/limited business loop.
- `EXCLUDE`: do not put in the main recommendation list.
